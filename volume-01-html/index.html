About This Volume

HTML (HyperText Markup Language) is the backbone of every website. It defines the structure and content of web pages — from headings and paragraphs to images, links, and forms. Think of HTML as the skeleton of a building: without it, nothing else can stand.

This volume walks you through the fundamentals of HTML from the ground up. Each chapter builds on the previous one, so it is recommended to follow them in order.

📑 Table of Contents

Chapter 1 The Document Structure Understanding <!DOCTYPE>, <html>, <head>, and <body>. How browsers read your code from top to bottom. Chapter 2 Page Architecture: Landmarks, Containers, and Identifiers Understanding Semantic landmarks like <header>, <main>, and <footer>, the role of generic containers like <div> and <span>, and using the class attribute as an identification bridge to CSS. Chapter 3 Text & Typography Headings (<h1>–<h6>), paragraphs, bold, italic, and the difference between inline and block elements. Chapter 4 Links & Navigation Anchor tags, relative vs absolute paths, internal page links, and opening links in new tabs. Chapter 5 Images & Media Embedding images with <img>, setting alt text for accessibility, and understanding PNG, JPG, and SVG formats. Chapter 6 Tables & Lists Ordered lists, unordered lists, definition lists, and building structured data tables with <table>. Chapter 7 Forms & Inputs Text fields, buttons, checkboxes, radio buttons, dropdowns, and how form submission works.
